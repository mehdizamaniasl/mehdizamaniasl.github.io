About Me

I am a PhD researcher at ZARM, University of Bremen. My research focuses on small-scale mixing in turbulent flows using experimental diagnostics and direct numerical simulations.

Research

My current work investigates passive-scalar mixing in an upscaled T-mixer using simultaneous PIV/PLIF measurements and numerical simulations.
