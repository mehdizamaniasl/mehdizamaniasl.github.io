Research Overview

My research focuses on the physical mechanisms governing small-scale mixing in turbulent flows. I combine advanced experimental diagnostics with direct numerical simulations to study the transport, deformation and dissipation of passive scalars.

My current PhD research at ZARM, University of Bremen, investigates mixing in an upscaled T-mixer. The work combines simultaneous particle image velocimetry and planar laser-induced fluorescence measurements with numerical simulations.

Experimental Investigation of a T-Mixer

I use simultaneous particle image velocimetry (PIV) and planar laser-induced fluorescence (PLIF) to measure the velocity and concentration fields in an upscaled T-mixer.

The main objectives are to:

  • Characterize the formation and deformation of scalar sheets.
  • Measure scalar variance and scalar-gradient statistics.
  • Examine turbulent scalar-flux transport.
  • Investigate the viscous-convective spectral range.
  • Assess the influence of inlet conditions on mixing.

Direct Numerical Simulation of T-Mixer Flow

Direct numerical simulations are performed using Nek5000 to investigate turbulent mixing at different Reynolds and Schmidt numbers. These simulations provide three-dimensional velocity and scalar fields that complement the planar experimental measurements.

The simulations are used to study:

  • Scalar variance and scalar-dissipation rates.
  • Schmidt-number effects on mixing dynamics.
  • Creation and deformation of scalar interfaces.
  • Effective diffusivity in the jet region.
  • Velocity-gradient and scalar-gradient alignment.

Homogeneous Isotropic Turbulence

I am also developing GPU-accelerated pseudo-spectral simulations of passive-scalar mixing in homogeneous isotropic turbulence. Large ensembles of simulations are used to investigate the statistical evolution of localized scalar structures.

This work focuses on scalar intermittency, mixing time, scalar-gradient amplification, geometrical alignment and the effects of the Schmidt number.

Research Methods

  • Particle image velocimetry (PIV)
  • Planar laser-induced fluorescence (PLIF)
  • Simultaneous velocity–scalar measurements
  • Direct numerical simulation using Nek5000
  • GPU-based pseudo-spectral simulation
  • Statistical and spectral turbulence analysis
